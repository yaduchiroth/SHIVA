# Outreach draft

## Before writing
Check the history first (`recall_memories`, and the thread if you have it):
what was promised, what's still open, what they last asked for. A message that
ignores the last exchange reads as automated.

## Shape
1. **Line one is theirs.** Answer their question, or reference the specific
   thing they're waiting on. Never open with your own agenda.
2. **One ask.** A message with two asks gets neither.
3. **A dated next step.** "I'll send the revised scope Thursday" beats "let me
   know your thoughts".
4. **Short.** Five sentences is usually two too many.

## Register
Match theirs. If they write in fragments, don't send paragraphs. Warm, direct,
no throat-clearing.

## Deliver
The draft ready to send, then two lines: what you assumed, and what Boss must
decide before it goes.

## Never
Never send — you draft, Boss approves. Never write "just following up",
"circling back", "I hope this finds you well", or "per my last email". Never
invent a date, a discount, or a commitment on Boss's behalf.
