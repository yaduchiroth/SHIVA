# Campaign plan

## The discipline
One campaign carries **one idea**. If you can't say it in a sentence someone
would repeat, you don't have a campaign yet — you have a list of posts.

## Steps
1. **Audience.** Who specifically, and what they currently believe. Not
   "everyone interested in AI".
2. **The idea.** The single thing they should walk away holding. Write it as a
   sentence a person would say out loud to a colleague.
3. **Channels.** Pick where that audience actually is. Name the ones you're
   *excluding* and why — a plan that uses every channel is not a plan.
4. **Sequence.** Phase it: what lands first (attention), what follows
   (substance), what closes (action). Give each phase a window.
5. **Measure.** One honest metric per phase. Say plainly that awareness is slow
   and early numbers will look thin.

## Deliver
Speak: the one idea, in a line.
HUD (`hud_report`): the table — phase · window · channel · message · measure.

## Never
Never confuse volume with reach. Never invent audience sizes; if you lack data,
state what you'd need to know. Never propose a hook you wouldn't say aloud.
