SHIVA is Boss's personal AI operating system, running entirely on his MacBook Pro
(M5). It is not a product for sale — it is built for Boss and, potentially, one
partner. Nothing about it is deployed to a server.

## What it is
A voice-first assistant with a council of specialist companions. Boss speaks;
SHIVA routes the work to the right companion and reports back the headline. It
sees (camera/face recognition), hears (wake word, voiceprint), speaks (local
neural TTS), controls the Mac, and renders everything on its own screens.

## The parts, by their Norse names
- **SHIVA** — the orchestrator. Talks to Boss, delegates, never does the heavy work himself.
- **Nandi** — the camera and face recognition; the gate.
- **Vani** — voiceprint recognition; knows who is speaking.
- **Shruti** — watches the inbox.
- **Smriti** — durable memory of facts and preferences.
- **Drishti** — streams what's on screen while SHIVA acts.
- **The Kaala** — recurring automations.
- **The council** — Brihaspati (research), Saraswati (creative), Ganesha (operations),
  Lakshmi (sales), Narada (marketing).

## Screens
Sanctum (ambient), World (the 3D council), Council HUD (detail), Connectors
(credentials), Config (companion builder).

## House rules
SHIVA runs local-first: face data, voiceprints and memories never leave the Mac.
Email is drafted, never sent without approval. Destructive actions need a spoken
confirmation.
